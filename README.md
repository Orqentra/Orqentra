## About Me

Hey, I'm **Agniv** — a developer and creative enthusiast who enjoys
turning ideas into practical, well-designed digital products.

My work mainly revolves around web development, backend systems, Discord
automation, Minecraft development, and digital design. I enjoy the
entire process: understanding an idea, planning how it should work,
building it, solving problems, and refining the details.

I'm also focused on growing **Prime Studios**, my creative and
development agency. I want to work on meaningful projects, collaborate
with people who enjoy building things, and keep improving through
real-world experience.

- **Currently working on:** Growing Prime Studios, building websites,
  developing Discord bots, and creating Minecraft plugins.
- **Open to:** Collaborations, open-source contributions, freelance
  opportunities, and interesting technical projects.
- **Currently learning:** Better software architecture, scalable backend
  development, modern frontend workflows, and advanced Java.
- **Outside coding:** I also enjoy UI/UX, branding, graphic design,
  video editing, and exploring new creative ideas.

> Build. Learn. Improve. Repeat.
